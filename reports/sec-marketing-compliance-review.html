01 · Executive summary

Overall assessment

PR #1 materially improved the biography, official branding, contact consistency, crawler metadata, and attorney / CPA scope boundaries without introducing new compliance concerns. We reviewed the intended production content package against the firm’s current Form ADV and Form CRS, Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-1 expectations, scope-of-practice language, privacy accuracy, and regulatory-link integrity. All checks listed in this memorandum pass. The content package is suitable for CCO marketing approval.

Recommendation to CCO Approve the Preserve My Estate website content package for public use under the firm’s advertising and marketing policies. Retain this memorandum and the Form ADV / Form CRS versions relied upon in the firm’s marketing advertising file.

02 · Scope of review

What was examined

Review covered the intended production content package after PR #1. DNS cutover, route publication, and CRM wiring are deployment operations outside this marketing-content approval.

Surfaces reviewed

  • Homepage — claims, proof row, biography, fit criteria, booking CTA, FAQ, footer
  • Calculator — estimate language, savings framing, method disclaimer
  • Guides (7) — educational framing, byline, not-attorney / not-advice disclaimers
  • Locations (13) — local claims, office details, CTA language
  • About — credentials, fiduciary language, booking ask
  • Legal — Privacy Policy, Website Disclosures, Terms of Use
  • Lead form — fields collected, consent language, promise accuracy
  • Regulatory links — Form ADV Part 2A/2B, Form CRS, IAPD firm summary
  • Supporting files — robots.txt, sitemap.xml, llms.txt, JSON-LD / schema.org identity fields

Standards applied

  • Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-1 — fair and balanced advertising; no untrue or misleading statements; substantiation of material claims
  • Form ADV / Form CRS consistency — website statements reconcilable to current filings
  • UPL / scope-of-practice — clear that the firm and featured IAR do not practice law
  • CFP Board mark usage — certification-mark attribution present
  • Privacy / truthful data practices — privacy policy matches actual form behavior
