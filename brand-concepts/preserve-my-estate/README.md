# Preserve My Estate logo concepts

Generated as early-stage identity explorations with the built-in image-generation workflow. The selected concept should be redrawn as production vector artwork before use.

The `-v2` boards are the current revision. They intentionally remove Michael Cammarata's name, credentials, MSA Financial, and all taglines from the logo itself. Those details belong in surrounding site content or regulatory disclosures, not in the brand mark.

## Shared brief

- Exact brand name: `Preserve My Estate`
- Logo text: `Preserve My Estate` only, with `PME` permitted as a compact secondary mark
- Palette: deep navy `#0D2B4E`, steel blue `#2D5B8E`, warm gold `#C4923A`, warm off-white `#FBF8F1`
- Tone: high-end editorial, restrained New England character, quietly confident, timeless, and approachable
- Requirements: strong silhouette, balanced negative space, favicon-to-signage scalability, and flat/vector-friendly construction
- Avoid: literal wealth, legal, house, lock, key, shield, and stock-market imagery

## Directions

1. **Editorial Wordmark** — typography-led identity with a fine gold rule and a compact `PME` shorthand.
2. **Continuity Monogram** — interlocking `P`, `M`, and `E` monogram paired with the full editorial wordmark.
3. **Preservation Frame** — contemporary open geometric symbol suggesting protection, structure, and room to grow.
