# Superseded MSA Financial logo concepts

> The brand name was subsequently confirmed as **Preserve My Estate**. The corrected concepts are in [`preserve-my-estate/`](preserve-my-estate/). These initial MSA boards remain here only as design-history references.

Generated as early-stage identity explorations with the built-in image-generation workflow. These PNG boards are for direction-setting only; the selected concept should be redrawn as production vector artwork before use.

## Shared brief

- Exact name: `MSA FINANCIAL`
- Descriptor: `FINANCIAL PLANNING · ASSET MANAGEMENT · SINCE 1997`
- Palette: deep navy `#0D2B4E`, steel blue `#2D5B8E`, warm gold `#C4923A`, warm off-white `#FBF8F1`
- Tone: high-end editorial, restrained New England wealth-management character, quietly confident and timeless
- Requirements: strong silhouette, balanced negative space, favicon-to-signage scalability, flat/vector-friendly construction
- Avoid: shields, crests, crowns, animals, coins, dollar signs, stock charts, bank buildings, roofs, trees, handshakes, gradients, shadows, bevels, and 3D effects

## Directions

1. **Heritage Monogram** — compact interlocking M/S/A letterform paired with an elegant serif wordmark and restrained gold accent.
2. **Editorial Wordmark** — typography-led identity with a proprietary gold crossbar detail in the A; no pictorial emblem.
3. **Structured Alignment** — geometric symbol representing planning, stewardship, and continuity, paired with editorial typography.
